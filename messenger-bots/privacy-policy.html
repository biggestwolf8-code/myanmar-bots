Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how the Biggest Seller Myanmar Bot ("the Bot") handles information when you message the Facebook Page The Biggest Gift Cards Seller Myanmar 3 on Messenger.

The Bot is an automated customer-service assistant run by the business behind the Page, which sells digital subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, ChatGPT, Canva, VPNs, and others). It answers common customer questions from a fixed product catalog and forwards anything it cannot answer to the business owner for a personal reply.

What we collect

  • Your page-scoped user ID (PSID). Facebook gives the Bot an ID that is unique to this Page. It is needed to send replies to your messages. It does not identify your Facebook profile outside this Page.
  • The content of messages you send to the Page. Text (product questions, prices, payment queries), button taps (catalog categories, quick replies), and photos (e.g. payment receipt screenshots) — everything the Bot needs to answer you.
  • Server logs. Webhook events and delivery statuses are written to the Bot's server log for troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To reply automatically with product information, prices, payment details, and warranty answers.
  • If the Bot cannot answer, your message is forwarded to the business owner, who replies personally in the same Messenger thread. A routing record (message ID to your PSID) is kept only to deliver that reply back to you.
  • Server logs are kept for troubleshooting and are not shared.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not use your messages for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any payment credentials through Messenger.

Data retention

Message routing records are kept only as long as needed to deliver replies and troubleshoot issues. The Bot does not maintain a customer database beyond this.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message the Page The Biggest Gift Cards Seller Myanmar 3 or contact @biggestseller1 on Telegram.
